Experience

Decades in games, embedded devices and data.

Founder-led, with a trusted network of senior engineers and specialists.

We've spent our careers shipping software where failure is visible: in players' hands, on embedded devices in people's homes, and in data that companies depend on.

Bluedawn was founded by Thanh Do, a principal engineer and former engineering manager with 21 years across all three fields, from architecting the toybox system behind Disney Infinity 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0 to building firmware for smart-home security cameras.

For bigger projects, we bring in senior engineers and specialists we've shipped with before, so you get a team sized to the work and led by someone who's done it.
