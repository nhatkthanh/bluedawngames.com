Gravity Swirl: privacy policy

Gravity Swirl is made by Bluedawn Games. Last updated: 14 November 2026

Your game data stays on your device

Your progress, coins, boosters and settings are saved only on your device. We have no accounts and no servers, and we never receive your name, email address or contacts. Uninstalling the game deletes this data. Settings → Reset progress erases it at any time; a purchase is kept.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • optional ads you choose to watch for a reward (extra moves or coins);
  • short ads between some levels.

To show and measure ads, Google collects data from your device, such as:

  • the advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location (derived from the IP address);
  • device and app information;
  • how you interact with ads.

Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & legal → “Ad privacy choices”.
  • iPhone: the game asks for your permission before ads may use your device's advertising identifier.
  • Your phone's settings: you can reset or limit the advertising ID there.
  • Content: ads are limited to family-friendly content.

Purchases

“Remove ads” is sold through Google Play or the App Store. They handle the payment; we only learn that the purchase was made, so the game can turn off the ads between levels.

Permissions

  • Internet access, for ads and purchases.
  • Vibration, for optional haptic feedback (Settings → Vibration).

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will update this policy, and say so in the game, before any change in how data is handled.
