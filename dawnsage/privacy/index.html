Dawnsage: privacy policy

Dawnsage is made by Bluedawn Games LLC. Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version

Dawnsage keeps everything on your phone. We have no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never see what you write.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • your star's name;
  • the moods you pick, and the teachings you receive, read and favourite;
  • your reflections and evening check-ins;
  • the intention you choose each morning, and the small actions and habits you tick off;
  • your points, levels and traits;
  • your settings, such as reminder times, theme, sky and focus.

It is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. If you add the home-screen widget, it shows your star's name and level and the day's teaching, never anything you wrote.

What leaves your phone

Nothing comes to us. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. When you share a teaching or export your data, your phone's share sheet sends it only where you choose.

If your phone backs up to iCloud or your Google account, that backup includes the app's data, as it does for most apps, so your star comes with you to a new phone.

Your choices

  • Reminders: turn them on or off and change their times in Settings.
  • Export: save everything as a file with Settings → Export my data.
  • Delete: erase everything with Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications, for the reminders you choose.
  • Alarms and reminders, so your reminders arrive at the minute you set.
  • Running at start-up, so your reminders still arrive after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration, for gentle feedback.

Dawnsage does not ask for internet access, your contacts, your location or your camera.

Children

Dawnsage is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with its date.
